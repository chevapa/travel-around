05 — The lexicon, locked

STATES Printed — been there Not printed — haven't Sub-state on printed: loved (colour) / fine (mono)
ACTIONS To Print — the wishlist verb and the “pick one for today” button New Frame — add a place Same roll — what's nearby
OBJECTS Frame — one place, any state The Atlas — the map view The Index — filters The Contact Sheet — album view
RULES Text never sits on a photo — captions live on the white border or an ink chip. Tilt budget ±4°, and only prints tilt — panels and the top bar never do. Prints shrink to a colour/mono/dashed square below 28px; tap target stays 44px.

References pull one way: hand-drawn riso geography, torn edges, real snapshots layered by hand. The atlas is the poster; the frames are the shoebox. ← back to the critique
