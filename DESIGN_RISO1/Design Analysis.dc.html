Design review · Travel Atlas · Атлас поездок

A great idea wearing three different outfits

The concept is genuinely good: a personal atlas of day trips out of Zagreb, with visited/liked state, a travel-time radius, and a “just tell me where to go” button. The riso-print identity is distinctive. What's holding it back is that the interface is a set of independent floating widgets — each styled by its own rules — dropped on top of a stock consumer basemap that fights the brand for attention.

01 — What's working

A real point of view

Riso magenta / yellow / teal on cream, hard offset shadows, cut-and-paste collage. Nobody will mistake this for a template. Protect it.

~1 ч ◠
The travel-time radius

Dashed isochrone rings labelled in hours are the single smartest thing here — day trips are chosen by drive time, not distance. It deserves far more prominence than it gets.

42 + 74
Diary vs. wishlist as one dataset

Places you've been and places you might go living on one map, distinguished by colour, is the right model. “Что рядом →” on the card is an excellent day-planning move.

02 — What's breaking, in priority order

■ broken header collage ■ four buttons, four visual languages
CRIT 01
The header block is visibly broken

The collage image overflows its cream card on three sides, the word “ЗАГРЕБ” is half-buried behind a starburst, and “31 МЕСТ” sits in a floating chip that appears to be escaping the container. It reads as a rendering bug, not as intentional collage — and it's the first thing anyone sees. (Also: “31 мест” should be “31 место”.)

Fix: give the collage a fixed aspect box with overflow:hidden, or drop the photo entirely and let the wordmark + place count stand alone. Deliberate overlap needs one element breaking the frame, not four.

CRIT 02
Filter panel text fails contrast badly

“Были, Понравилось”, “Были, Нейтрально” and “Ещё не были” are set in pale grey on cream — roughly 2.4:1, against a 4.5:1 requirement. The counts and the “только” links beside them are lighter still. These are the primary controls of the whole product and they read as disabled.

Fix: ink #14140F for every filter label, #6b6455 as the absolute lightest for counts. Show unselected state with the checkbox, never by fading the label.

HIGH 03
The basemap is competing with your pins

A stock basemap at full saturation puts bright green forest, blue lakes and pink motorways under magenta pins. The roads are nearly the same hue as your primary marker colour, so pins stop being figure and become part of the ground. The purple “not visited” dots — your largest category at 74 places — are the worst off: dark violet on mid-green is almost invisible at a glance.

Fix: a custom cream/greige basemap — desaturate landcover to ~10%, roads to warm grey, keep water a pale tint, drop POI icons. All colour saturation on the map should belong to your data.

HIGH 04
Marker hierarchy is inverted, and there's no legend

Clusters are big, magenta and yellow-ringed; individual places are small, dark and quiet. So the loudest things on the map are the ones carrying the least information — “2” tells you nothing. Meanwhile pin colour encodes status (teal / orange / purple) and nothing on the map explains what those colours mean; the key is buried inside a panel you have to open.

Fix: mute clusters to outlined cream-on-ink and give single places the colour and weight. Put a permanent 3-line legend in a bottom corner — it doubles as a one-tap filter.

HIGH 05
Four buttons, four different visual languages

“КУДА?” is yellow-filled, “СБРОСИТЬ” and “+ МЕСТО” are white outlined, “ФИЛЬТРЫ” is black-filled — with no relationship between colour and importance. Black “ФИЛЬТРЫ” currently shouts louder than the primary action. The search field, which is the least-used control on a map you browse visually, is the widest element in the bar. And the toolbar floats detached from the header block, so the app has two unrelated pieces of chrome.

Fix: one bar, one hierarchy — yellow = the single primary action, ink = secondary, quiet = tertiary. Shrink search to an icon that expands.

MED 06
The place card buries its own headline

Five metadata chips — status, country, and three category tags with emoji — occupy the top third of the card, above the place name. The name is what you came for. Worse, the card floats unanchored in the middle of the map with no tail or connector, so you lose track of which pin you tapped, and it covers the surroundings you'd want to see. The status chip also restates a filter you already set.

Fix: name → one-line description → drive time → tags at the bottom. Anchor the card to its pin with a pointer, or dock it as a side panel / bottom sheet so the map stays legible.

MED 07
Panels stack instead of coexisting

Open the filters with a card showing and you get two overlapping sheets plus a header — three layers of chrome over the map, with the filter panel running off the bottom edge and its footer (“31 МЕСТ ПОДХОДИТ / ПО РАССТОЯНИЮ”) partly cut. Filters also open with every section collapsed and no indication of what's currently active, so you must expand each one to find out.

Fix: one panel slot — opening filters replaces the card. Cap the panel at viewport height with an internal scroll and a pinned footer. Show active filters as removable chips on the collapsed section header.

MED 08
The counts don't add up, and the type is fighting Cyrillic

The header says 31 places; the status filter shows 31 / 11 / 74; the footer says 31 match — but only “Ещё не были” (74) is checked. Three different 31s that mean different things. Separately, the display face's question mark renders as a reversed glyph in “КУДА?”, and its Cyrillic caps are noticeably wider and weaker than the Latin — the wordmark and the UI labels are effectively two different typefaces.

Fix: label every number (“показано 31 из 116”). Keep the display face for the wordmark only; set UI in a face with first-class Cyrillic.

03 — Redesign proposal

Same identity, same features, restructured: one bar of chrome across the top, one panel slot on the right, a quiet basemap, and a permanent legend. Nothing below is a new feature — it's the existing product with the hierarchy sorted out.

One bar, one hierarchy

Wordmark, honest counts, and actions in ascending weight: icon → outline → ink → yellow. Only “Куда сегодня?” gets the offset shadow, so the primary action is unambiguous. Filters carry a badge for how many are active.

Legend as a control

The three statuses live permanently in the corner with their counts. It explains the colour code and doubles as the filter you actually use 90% of the time — tap a row to isolate it.

Card leads with the name

Name, description, then a scannable facts table — drive time is the number you decide on, so it stops being body text. Tags drop to the bottom. Docked right, it never covers the pin or its surroundings.

Quiet ground, loud data

Cream basemap, warm-grey roads, no stock POIs. Clusters become outlined counters; single places keep the saturated status colour, and the isochrone rings finally read.

04 — If you only do four things

  1. 01Darken every filter label to full ink — one CSS change, biggest usability gain.
  2. 02Clip the header collage, or delete the photo. It currently looks broken.
  3. 03Desaturate the basemap so the pins are the only saturated thing on screen.
  4. 04Add the corner legend. Colour is doing real work with nothing to decode it.
