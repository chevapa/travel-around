The Atlas's ground: torn photo fragments sunk under flat riso ink washes. Never a stock map tile.

```jsx
<div style={{ position: 'relative' }}>
  <TornGround fragments={['assets/collage2.webp', 'assets/collage.jpeg', 'assets/photo-stack.jpeg']} />
  {/* prints, rings, chrome, then <GrainOverlay /> */}
</div>
```

The washes are what make it legible: without the blue/green/yellow multiply layers the collage competes with the Prints. Keep it absolutely positioned as the first child of the Atlas container.
